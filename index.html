Questions

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC and a TikTok account you go LIVE with. For stream overlays, OBS Studio or TikTok LIVE Studio.

How does the licence work?

After subscribing you get a licence key. Enter it once and it activates on that PC. It works offline for a while if your internet drops. To move to a new PC, remove it from the old one first (Settings → Licence) and activate on the new one.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime from the “Manage subscription” page. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. See our refund policy.

Does it work with OBS?

Yes. The overlays are browser sources that work in OBS Studio and TikTok LIVE Studio.

Is my data private?

Your setups stay on your PC. Licence checks only send your key and an anonymous PC ID. See the privacy policy.
