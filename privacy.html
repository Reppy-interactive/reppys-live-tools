Privacy policy

Reppys Interactive LIVE Tools, by Reppy's Interactives.

What stays on your PC

Your setups — actions, events, sounds, overlays, profiles and settings — are stored on your own computer. We don't upload or collect them.

Licence checks

When the app checks your licence, it sends your licence key and an anonymous ID for your PC (a one-way hash — we can't turn it back into anything about you or your hardware) to our licence server. That lets us tie a key to one PC and keep it working. We store the key's status and the app version it last reported.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe processes your payment information under Stripe's privacy policy.

Services the app talks to

Depending on what you use, the app contacts third parties directly from your PC: GitHub (to check for updates), and services you connect such as TikTok, Spotify or Apple Music. Those services handle your data under their own policies.

What we don't do

We don't run analytics or ad tracking in the app. Note that our website (GitHub Pages) and our payment and licence providers may log your IP address as part of serving requests, as is normal for web services.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or to ask about data tied to your licence: reppysinteractive@gmail.com.
